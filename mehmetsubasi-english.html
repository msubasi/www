I am, Mehmet Subaşı.


Patriot / Science Enthusiast
Digital Economy Advocate

1970
Istanbul
Born.

B.S. in Engineering, UT Austin; MBA from Columbia University.

  • I completed my secondary education at Alman Lisesi (the German High School) and Semiha Şakir High School, then earned a degree in Mechanical and Industrial Engineering from the University of Texas at Austin. I subsequently pursued graduate studies at Columbia University in New York City, completing my MBA and closing the foundational chapter of my academic journey.

I provided management consulting services to the world's largest corporations.

After beginning my career as an Industrial Engineer at Ford Motor Company in Detroit, I transitioned into management consulting, joining globally respected firms such as Monitor Group and Bain & Co. Still in my twenties, I had the opportunity to advise senior executives at some of the world's largest corporations across Boston, Milan, and London — gaining deep insight into complex corporate operations and C-suite perspectives at an early age.

I embraced digital as early as the 1990s.

After returning to Turkey, I founded Vodaco Marketing Strategies, a consultancy focused on the impact of the digital revolution on businesses, and served as Chairman of Vodaco Agency, its full-service digital marketing arm. I have spoken and trained at numerous conferences on marketing, digital marketing, online public relations, social media, blockchain technology, and the future of financial services. I have also authored multiple publications in these fields and served as a faculty member in marketing at Bilgi University.

Social responsibility is the passion that enriches my life.

Whenever time allows, I dedicate myself to social responsibility initiatives — particularly in technology and education. As Chairman of the Board of the Turkey Science Centers Foundation, I worked to foster a love of science among young people across Turkey. I also served on the advisory board of the Computer Engineering Department at Boğaziçi University to help strengthen Turkey's strategic capabilities. Over the years, I have served as chairman and board member of numerous civil society organizations, supporting a wide range of social responsibility projects. Beyond my Galatasaray Club membership, I have contributed to several industry associations. My intellectual interests extend to philosophy and political systems. I am happily married and the proud father of two kind-hearted children.

Politics

Vatan Party (Patriotic Party)

Eyüpsultan Municipal Mayoral Candidate

Without wishing to boast, I am a proud member of the Vatan Party (Patriotic Party). In recent years, serving my country as a committed representative of my party has been a tremendous source of honor. In the 2023 general elections, I stood as a parliamentary candidate for Istanbul on behalf of the Vatan Party. In the 2024 local elections, I ran as the Vatan Party's candidate for Mayor of Eyüpsultan — the district I have called home for nearly 15 years.
